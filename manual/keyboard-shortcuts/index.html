Color labels

KeyAction
6Red
7Yellow
8Green
9Blue
⇧6 – 9Label, don't advance

This table shows the Lightroom convention, which is the default. Choose Color label keys: Phocus in Settings (⌘,) to remap these keys to 6 green, 7 yellow, 8 red, with no key for blue. See Rating and Flagging.
