Palomino.

Fast, focused RAW photo culling for macOS.

Palomino does one thing: get you through a large shoot quickly — rating keepers, skipping rejects — so you can hand a tight selection to your editing software. No editing, no organizing, no cloud sync. Just culling, as fast as possible.

Why it's fast

⚡︎Embedded previews

Reads the camera-written JPEG preview inside every RAW file instead of decoding sensor data — 10–100× faster, indistinguishable at viewing distance.

⌨Keyboard-first

1–5 to rate, P to pick, X to reject — every action auto-advances. Your hands never leave the keys, and there's an overlay (?) when you forget one.

◎Quality hints

Background analysis flags overexposed, underexposed, and out-of-focus shots, and clusters bursts so you can keep the best frame and reject the rest in one key.

⇆Plays well with others

Ratings and labels are written to standard XMP sidecars, so your work round-trips with any XMP-aware editing tool. Sessions resume instantly where you left off.
